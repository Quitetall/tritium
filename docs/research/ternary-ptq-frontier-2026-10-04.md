# Ternary PTQ frontier and Tritium gap assessment

**Research date:** 2026-10-04
**Checkout inspected:** `045236482c1770e1980b90b17607052cfc7613c9`
**Scope:** primary-source scan of 2024–2026 ternary/ultra-low-bit LLM PTQ and refinement; source inspection of ADR 0033, ADR 0028, proposed ADR 0034, plan 0043, and the Python PTQ/refinement implementation. No model artifacts were downloaded and no benchmark or campaign was run.
**Bottom line:** Tritium has a strong, unusually explicit additive-ternary research contract, but source code and empirical evidence do not yet establish that its current converter is SOTA. Its current public Python PTQ fitter is a useful deterministic baseline, not yet the joint output-aware SALT V2 method specified by the research plan. Keep “SOTA” **unknown** until matched artifacts pass the frozen gates.

## What “SOTA” needs to mean here

There is no single winner across different representations and objectives. Compare at least three distinct questions:

1. **Pure additive ternary:** Are deployed matrix values only `sum_p scale[p] * trit[p]`, with trits in `{-1,0,+1}` and no floating residual, offset, or arbitrary decoder? Compare methods at equal exact artifact/resident bytes.
2. **Best compressed quality regardless of representation:** Compare against methods that spend extra bits on salient residuals, factorized ternary matrices, or multilevel codebooks. These are important product baselines, but do not count as pure additive-ternary wins.
3. **End-to-end product quality and speed:** Compare the reloaded artifact, on the intended kernels and full task/runtime harness. Layer reconstruction error and author-reported nominal “1.58-bit” rates cannot establish this.

ADR 0028 already requires zero-point-free additive planes, physical-byte accounting, and separate additive-ternary versus global low-bit Pareto claims. ADR 0033 makes that discipline part of the public-release claim boundary. Plan 0043 names strong baselines and a strict campaign sequence. These are sound guardrails; papers do not waive them.

## Checkout comparison: implemented behavior vs planned SOTA

- **Implemented Python PTQ path:** [`ptq.py`](../../crates/tritium-py/python/tritium/torch/ptq.py) captures and admits calibration evidence and fits one-to-three additive planes. `_diagonal_additive_projection` uses a per-input-feature nonnegative diagonal weight, initializes planes greedily from the residual, then runs 20 per-plane coordinate/scale updates. This is a valuable deterministic reference and uses actual scale-group geometry, but it does not perform the plan’s joint enumeration of all `3^P` coordinate states, block GPTQ/LDL error propagation, delta correction after refits, or a full output-KL/Fisher matrix solve.
- **Capture is not the same as solver adoption:** the module capture helpers include Qwen-specific and Kronecker-evidence paths; the inspected converter’s fit objective reads a vector curvature record and passes it to the diagonal additive projection. A richer capture artifact is not evidence that the fit consumes the richer objective.
- **Refinement is narrower than the frontier:** [`refinement.py`](../../crates/tritium-py/python/tritium/torch/refinement.py) implements scale-only refinement over fixed hard planes. This is useful and format-preserving, but does not by itself implement QTEA’s by-column trit refit/error-decay, CAT-Q’s sliding-layer output fit, HESTIA’s sensitivity-scheduled soft relaxation, or an end-to-end hard-trit recovery stage.
- **QAT is distinct from PTQ:** [`estimators.py`](../../crates/tritium-py/python/tritium/torch/estimators.py) exposes additive STE/annealed/Hestia-style estimator infrastructure and TWN/TTQ/sparse estimator entries. These support differentiable training research; they do not make a PTQ converter SOTA or substitute for a validated recipe and exported-model gates.
- **Contract strength:** ADR 0028 and plan 0043 prescribe several techniques beyond the current module converter, including guided-Fisher/forward-KL curvature, OA-EM multi-start initialization, conditioned nonnegative scale fitting, block feedback, exact-byte allocation, and global output/task gates. ADR 0033 explicitly says the v1.1 public platform and Qwen empirical gates remain open. The contract is ambitious, but it is not implementation or qualification evidence.

## Practical improvement sequence

1. **Keep the accepted frozen gates.** Do not relax the physical-byte, coverage, held-out, task-retention, runtime, or independent-review requirements to obtain a quick green result.
2. **Close the highest-value algorithm gap in the current supported path:** implement and test joint `3^P` trit assignment with conditioned nonnegative scale solving, deterministic restarts, and monotonic objective receipts. Compare against current diagonal-greedy fitting on fixed synthetic goldens, then the approved small-model rung.
3. **Make curvature end-to-end:** bind the calibration objective and output-curvature artifact to the fit receipt; implement separate diagonal, guided-Fisher, and forward-KL/Kronecker solver arms. A test should fail if a requested richer arm silently falls back to diagonal curvature.
4. **Add block feedback and exact delta propagation**, with a small independent reference and constructed regression fixture. Then make plane selection optimize exact encoded bytes, not a nominal trit count.
5. **Treat recent methods as isolated baselines/ablations:** PTQTP and BPDQ for close optimizer comparisons; QTEA for the quality frontier including separately charged sparse FP8 residuals; TWLA for rotation/asymmetric fitting and, in a separate W1.58A4 study, activation co-design; ExTernD for a clearly separate factorized quality/compute frontier. Avoid combining multiple new methods before causal attribution.
6. **Separate empirical truth from software readiness:** first establish the small-model recipe and artifact/replay path; only then use the frozen Qwen campaign and report model-quality, rate, runtime, and memory results. Independent reviewers must inspect full artifacts and receipts. Until those gates pass, additive-ternary SOTA and global low-bit Pareto status remain **UNKNOWN**.

The list above is an assessment, not an amendment to the accepted ADR or permission to start expensive model work. Any change to a frozen public contract must follow the repository’s ADR/amendment rules.
