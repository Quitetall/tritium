# Code of Conduct

Tritium is a technical community for ternary-model research and engineering.
Participation requires professional, evidence-oriented collaboration.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful across differences in experience, identity, language, and
  technical opinion.
- Critique claims, code, experiments, and decisions—not people.
- State uncertainty, disclose relevant conflicts, and correct errors openly.
- Make benchmarks reproducible and label negative results, limitations, and
  unsupported configurations honestly.
- Respect privacy, embargoed security reports, model licenses, dataset terms,
  and requests to stop unwanted contact.

Harassment, threats, discrimination, sexualized attention, doxxing, sustained
disruption, plagiarism, benchmark manipulation, and retaliation are not
acceptable in project spaces or when representing the project.

## Reporting and enforcement

For ordinary conduct concerns not involving the maintainer, contact
`briankhanglam@gmail.com`. If the concern involves the sole maintainer, do not
send it to the subject: use
[GitHub's private abuse-reporting route](https://support.github.com/contact/report-abuse).
Project-level adjudication is handled by the maintainer named in
[GOVERNANCE.md](GOVERNANCE.md), who will recuse and escalate to GitHub's abuse
route for any report involving themselves; immediate platform safety measures may
proceed in either case. Security
vulnerabilities follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md). Do not publish sensitive
personal or vulnerability details in an issue.

An unconflicted maintainer may remove content, limit participation, or ban a participant.
Enforcement should be proportionate, documented privately, and separate from
technical disagreement. A maintainer involved in a complaint must seek a
neutral reviewer before deciding it; if none is available, the conflict and
interim safety action must be disclosed to the affected parties.

Project-controlled spaces include the repository, issue tracker, pull requests,
and any future channel explicitly linked by the repository. No unlisted chat or
social account is an official reporting route.
